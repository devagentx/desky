## Purchasing rules

1. Validate component voltage and peak-current requirements before ordering.
2. Prefer modules with accessible documentation and local availability.
3. Do not substitute a weaker motor driver solely to reduce cost.
4. Use protected battery cells and a charger intended for the selected chemistry.
5. Buy the minimum parts needed for the next experiment, not the final enclosure.
